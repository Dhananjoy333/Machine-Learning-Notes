The origins of calculus starts from a simple shape of curve more specifically circle. Early mathematicians, could not find the are inside curves as there were no measuring instruments that can accurately measure curves.

![](circle.svg)

To find the area of circle we have cut the circle into several concentric circles. Now if we take any one of the pieces and flatten it out :

![](circle-line%20area.svg)

Area of the flatten line is approximately  $2\pi r\mathrm{d}r$ . Here smaller the $\mathrm{d}r$ width more accurate the area will be. Now if we take all the concentric circles and put it on number line :

![](circ-tri.svg)

Here the last line is of height $2\pi r$ .Here as size of $\mathrm{d}r$ gets less and less the area inside start resembling more and more like a triangle.

![](smaller-dr.svg)

And we know area of triangle is = $\frac{1}{2}bh$ . So here :
$$
	\begin{array}{c}
	\text{Area of triangle}&= \frac{1}{2}bh \\
	&=\frac{1}{2}r2\pi r \\
	&=\pi r^{2} \\
	\text{So, Area of circle is}&= \pi r^{2}
	\end{array}
$$
