- Design A has more customers because it is old design. Usually in most such testing the new design has lower no. of people to him it is introduced. Because if design B is really bad we could lose already existing customer.
- Design A has sample size = 80 and average purchase is 50 with sample standard deviation = 10. Design B has sample size = 20 and average purchase is 55 with sample standard deviation = 15. We usually do 80-20 split.
- Simply looking at average purchase we might say Design B has more purchase, as it has on average 5 more but is it significant enough to determine if it is actually a better design.
