Up until now we have dealt with 1 independent sample and 2 independent sample now we have another case of dependent sample

![](../assets/Hypothesis%20testing_3/paired.svg)

Here If we take a sample of people and put those people through a training program and after few weeks/months we look at the same people and ask the 3 test questions : 
- Has the training program improved them? (Right tailed test)
- Has the training program deteriorate them? (Left tailed test)
- Has they experienced any difference from before? (Two tailed test).
Here each physical person is the same before and after the training. Hence, when collecting data, and comparing the comparison must be done for same person before and after. Hence the person data is paired with its past and present data.  
To see the difference for a person we simply subtract the present and past data for that particular person

![](../assets/Hypothesis%20testing_3/paired_1.svg)

population standard deviation being unknown we take the t-statistics. Finding the t value

![](../assets/Hypothesis%20testing_3/paired_2.svg)

Now we can conduct the test, for right tailed test :
- Null Hypothesis, $H_{o}$ : Nothing has changed.
- Alternate Hypothesis, $H_{a}$ : Condition Improved.
- Significance level, $\alpha$ : 0.05
