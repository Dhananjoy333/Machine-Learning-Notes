## Moments of Distribution

**moments** are quantitative measures that describe the shape and features of a probability distribution.

![](../assets/moments,%20skewness%20and%20kurtosis/moments.svg)

Here $\mathbb{E}[X]$ is called expectation but it can also be called $1st$ moment. Similarly,
- $\mathbb{E}[X^2]$ - $2nd$ moments. 
- $\mathbb{E}[X^3]$ - $3rd$ moments. 
- $\mathbb{E}[X^n]$ - $nth$ moments. 
## Skewness

![](../assets/moments,%20skewness%20and%20kurtosis/same%20ex,var.svg)

In the above distribution both expectation and variance or $1st$ and $2nd$ moment of distribution are same. But we can clearly see the distributions are vastly different. As one is mirror image of other. To differentiate between both we use the $3rd$ moment of distribution also called skewness

![](../assets/moments,%20skewness%20and%20kurtosis/3rd%20moment.svg)

![](../assets/moments,%20skewness%20and%20kurtosis/skew.svg)

Here 
- **Positively/right-skewed:** the distribution has a **long tail extending to the right** → a few unusually large values pull the distribution toward the right.
- **Negatively/left-skewed:** the distribution has a **long tail extending to the left** → a few unusually small values pull it toward the left.
- **Not skewed:** roughly symmetric.
## Kurtosis

![](../assets/moments,%20skewness%20and%20kurtosis/same%203rd%20moment.svg)

**Kurtosis** tells you about the **extremeness of the tails** of a distribution — in other words, how prone the distribution is to producing **outliers/extreme values**.
