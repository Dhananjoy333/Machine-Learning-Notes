In the above cases Expectation cannot differentiate the two cases. But clearly the two cases are not same as one has more spread than other. This is where variance comes in, variance help us in determining the spread in a distribution.

It is calculated as sum of square of difference between a value and mean.

$$
	\sigma^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_{i}-\mu)^2
$$

## Standard Deviation

Variance is a good measure of spread but it requires squaring the values this results in mismatch of units. As all measurements may be taken in $m$ but variance will be in $m^2$ . To solve this we simply take the square root of the solution of variance this is called standard deviation.

![](../assets/variance%20and%20SD/standard%20deviation.svg)

## 68-95-99.7 Rule

In a normal distribution,
- $68\%$ of the data lie in 1 standard deviation far from mean.
- $95\%$ lie in 2 standard deviation far from mean.
- $99.7\%$  lie in 3 standard deviation far from mean.
