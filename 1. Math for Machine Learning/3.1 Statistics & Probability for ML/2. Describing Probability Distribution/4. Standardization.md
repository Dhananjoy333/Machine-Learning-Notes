While working with datasets it is much easier if the mean is 0 and standard deviation is 1. The process by which we bring the mean of a dataset to 0 and standard deviation to 1 is called standardization.

- Centering Mean : To center the mean to 0 we simply subtract each value in dataset by mean

![](../assets/variance%20and%20SD/centering%20mean.svg)

- Centering Standard Deviation : To center standard deviation we simply divide the dataset by standard deviation.

![](../assets/variance%20and%20SD/centering%20SD.svg)
