Calculating the probability of an event

$$
	P(E)=\frac{\text{no. of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total no. of possible outcome}}
$$

## Compliment of Probability

The probability of an event not happening is calculated by :

$$
	P(E')=1-P(E)
$$
## Sum of Probability (Disjoint Event)

Events that cannot happen at same time is called Disjoint Events

Q1. In a school of 10 students. Student are given a choice to choose one sport from : Football, Cricket, and chess. Each student can choose only one sport. 3 chose football, 2 chose cricket and 5 chess. What is the the probability that a student chosen at random chose football or chess?

$$
	P(F\cup C)=P(F)+P(C)=\frac{3}{10}+\frac{5}{10}=\frac{8}{10}
$$
## Sum of Probability (Joint Event)

Events that can happen at same time is called joint Events

Q1. In a school of 10 students. Student are given a choice to choose a sport : Football, basketball or both. 6 chose football, 5 chose basketball , 3 chose both and 2 chose none. Find probability of student choosing football or basketball?

$$
	P(F\cup B)=P(F)+P(B)-P(F\cap B)=\frac{6}{10}+\frac{5}{10}-\frac{3}{10}=\frac{8}{10}
$$

## Product Rule (Independent events)

Independent events are things that happen where the result of the first one does not change the odds of the second one.

$$
	P(A\cap B)=P(A).P(B)
$$
## Conditional Probability

Probability of an event happening, given another event already happened.

$$
	P(B|A)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}
	
$$
