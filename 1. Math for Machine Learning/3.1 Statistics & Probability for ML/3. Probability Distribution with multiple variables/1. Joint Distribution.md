## Continuous Distribution

Earlier in continuous distribution we have seen case of waiting time before call and also learning the probability between an interval is simply the area under the curve of that interval.

![](../assets/Joint%20Distribution/cont.%20ex.svg)

Here we take the same case but add another random variable $Y$ which shows customer rating and we draw individual graphs.

![](../assets/Joint%20Distribution/individual%20cont.%20graph.svg)

Now we combine the two graph datapoints into a single scatter plot, we observe that 
- Customer rating is very high if waiting time is very low.
- Customer rating is very low if waiting time is very high.

![](../assets/Joint%20Distribution/cont.%20X-Y%20scatter.svg)

For the same scatter plot we can also draw the heatmap

![](../assets/Joint%20Distribution/heatmap.svg)
