## Marginal Distribution

If we have a joint distribution of 2 random variable and now we only care about one random variable, the distribution we derive from joint distribution for one variable while ignoring the other is called marginal distribution

Example - 1 :
Joint distribution of children ages and heights

![](../assets/marginal%20and%20conditional%20dist/age-height%20dist.svg)

In order to find the marginal distribution we simply add all values w.r.t. concerned rows and cols

![](../assets/marginal%20and%20conditional%20dist/marginal%20dist.svg)

![](../assets/marginal%20and%20conditional%20dist/marginal_dist.svg)

Example - 2 : 
Two die roll and random variables being the value obtained in each die 

![](../assets/marginal%20and%20conditional%20dist/die%20roll_1.svg)

Example - 3 :
Two die roll and one random variable being value obtained in first die and second random variable being sum of first and second die 

![](../assets/marginal%20and%20conditional%20dist/die%20roll_2.svg)

Example - 4 : (continuous distribution)
Call waiting time and customer rating

![](../assets/marginal%20and%20conditional%20dist/call%20wait.svg)

If the joint distribution is represented by a 3D graph, marginal distribution is simply taking a plane and moving along a respective axis.
