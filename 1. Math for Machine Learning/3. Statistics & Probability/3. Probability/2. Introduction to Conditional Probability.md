Conditional probability is the probability of an event occurring given that another event has already happened.

$$
\begin{align}
P(A|B)&=\text{Probability of A given B} \\ \\
\text{We know for } &\text{dependent events A and B} \\ \\
P(A\cap B)&=P(A).P(B|A) \\ &or\\
P(B\cap A)&=P(B).P(A|B) \\
&\text{So}, \\
 \\
P(B|A)&=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}
 \\
&or \\
 \\
P(A|B)&=\frac{P(B\cap A)}{P(B)}
 \\ \\
\text{Note: } P(A\cap B)&=P(B\cap A)
\end{align}
$$

Q. Experiment of rolling 2 dice.
- A : Getting 4 on 1st die.
- B : Sum of outcome is 6.
Find the probability that sum of outcome is 6 given that outcome of first die is 4.
Ans:
- A = {(4, 1); (4, 2); (4, 3); (4, 4); (4, 5); (4, 6)}
- B = {(1, 5); (2, 4); (3, 3); (4, 2); (5, 1)}
Here,
- Given first value is 4, $P(A\cap B)$ is only possible with (4, 2)
- So, $P(A\cap B)=\frac{1}{36}$
- and $P(A)=\frac{6}{36}$
Therefore,

$$
	P(B|A)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}=\frac{\frac{1}{36}}{\frac{6}{36}}=\frac{1}{6}
	
$$

## Intuitive Understanding

$$
	P(A|B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
$$

2. Once event B is confirmed to have occurred all outcomes outside event B is discarded.

![](../assets/2.%20Probability/event_B_occurred.svg)

3. Now for Event A to also be true all outcomes must lie inside $A\cap B$.

$$
	\begin{align}
	P(A|B)&=\frac{n(A\cap B)}{n(B)} \\ \\
	
	\text{Since,} \\ \\
	
	P(A) &= \frac{n(A)}{n(S)},P(A)\leq 1 \\ \\ 
	 
	\text{dividing by n(S) to both numerator and denominator,} \\
	 \\
	P(A|B)&=\frac{\frac{n(A\cap B)}{n(S)}}{\frac{n(B)}{n(S)}} \\
	 \\
	&=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
	\end{align}
$$

Hence, for mutually exclusive events since there is no $A\cap B$ . So $P(A|B)$ or $P(B|A)$ is always 0.

## Properties of Conditional Probability

1. $P(B|B)=1$
2. If $A_{1},A_{2},A_{3}$ are mutually exclusive events
$$
		P(A_{1}\cap A_{2}\cap A_{3}|B)=P(A_{1}|B)+P(A_{2}|B)+P(A_{3}|B)
	$$
3. $P(S|A)=1$
4. $P(\bar{A}|B)=1-P(A|B)$
5. $P(A\cup B|C)=P(A|C)+P(B|C)-P(A\cap B|C)$
6. If $A\subset B$ then $P(A|C)\leq P(B|C)$
7. Chain Rule for Conditional probability
