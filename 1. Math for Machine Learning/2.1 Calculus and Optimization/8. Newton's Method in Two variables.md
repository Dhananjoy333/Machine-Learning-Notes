In two variables or more Newton's Method require hessian matrix

![](assets/newton's%20method%20in%202%20var/2%20var%20newton's%20method.svg)

Or more appropriately we need inverse of hessian matrix

When doing the operations , a note to keep in mind is to not multiply  first derivative to hessian matrix it will cause matrix shape error

![](assets/newton's%20method%20in%202%20var/correct%20order.svg)
