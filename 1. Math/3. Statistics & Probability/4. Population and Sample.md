**Population** is the whole group of people, items or element of Interest.
**Example:**
- All students in a school.
- All voters in a country.
- Every tree in a forest.

A **sample** is a smaller group selected from the population to represent it.
**Example:**
- 100 students chosen from a school of 2,000 students.
- 1,000 voters surveyed from a country of millions of voters.
- 50 trees examined from a forest of 10,000 trees.

**Usage** :  If we want to find average height of adults in India ?
- Population : Every citizen of India above 18+ is our population for this survey. But it is not feasible to go around and measure the height of every adult citizen of India.
- Sample : We take a smaller number of citizen that are 18+ and calculate the average. This selected citizens are our sample for the survey.
