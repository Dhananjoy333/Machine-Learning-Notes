Q. In a bucket containing 4 red and 3 white balls, 2 balls are drawn at random one at a time without replacement.
- A : Event of drawing a red ball in 1st attempt.
- B : Event of drawing a red ball in 2nd attempt.
What is the probability of event B occurring ? 
Ans : 
- Here Event B is dependent on event A. 
- If event A is success we have 1 less red ball decreasing the probability of getting red ball again the second time i.e., event B.
- If event A is failure all red ball are still in bucket and 1 white ball is less, increasing the probability of getting red ball the second time i.e., event B.
$$
\begin{align}
P(B)&=P(A\cap B) + P(\bar{A}\cap B) \\
 \\
&=P(A).P(B|A) + P(\bar{A}).P(B|\bar{A}) \\
 \\
&=\frac{4}{7}.\left( \frac{3}{6} \right)+\frac{3}{7}.\left( \frac{4}{6} \right)
\end{align}
$$
